# Per-joint torque limits (first principles)

Generated by `torque_limits.py` from `blur/descriptor.json` (KV, 16:1 ratio,
driver currents). Output torque: `τ = (9.5493/KV)·gear·η·I`. Primary table is
at **η = 0.75** (the brief's assumption); `--eta 0.85` reproduces the
descriptor's numbers as a cross-check.

Nothing here is a measurement. Every "verified: false" figure must be
confirmed on load day with a lever arm and a scale.

## Driver current ratings used

| driver | motors | peak A | continuous (RMS) A | source |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| B-G431B-ESC1 | 12 × 2806.5 legs | 40 | ~6 (placeholder) | ST: 40 A is a **forced-air peak**, not continuous; no official continuous figure — measure. |
| SimpleFOC Mini (DRV8313) | 7 × 4015, 6 × 2204, 2 winch | 2.5 | 1.75 | TI DRV8313 datasheet: 2.5 A peak / 1.75 A RMS per output. |
| DRV8833 | N20 gripper | 2.0 | 1.5 | TI DRV8833 datasheet: 2 A peak / 1.5 A RMS per H-bridge. |

## Deliverable torque (η = 0.75)

| joint group | motor | design N·m | continuous N·m | peak N·m | limited by |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| abduction ×4, spine S1/twist/S2 (the 7 × 4015) | 4015 @ DRV8313 | 3.80 | **2.00** | **2.87** | driver current |
| leg pitch/knee/dist ×12 | 2806.5 @ ESC1 | 4.00 | 0.53\* | 3.53 | thermal\* / driver |
| neck lift/pan/tilt, jaw | 2204 @ DRV8313 | 0.80 | 0.77 | 0.80 | driver / mechanical |
| tail winches ×2 | 2204 winch | (cable) | 0.77 | 1.10 | driver |
| gripper | N20 @ DRV8833 | 0.30 | 0.30 | 0.30 | mechanical |

\* The 2806.5 legs are **not** current-ceiling limited at peak (40 A reaches
3.5 N·m, near the 4.0 mechanical cap); they are **thermally** limited for
sustained torque. The 0.53 N·m "continuous" uses a conservative 6 A no-fan
placeholder; the real sustained limit is governed by the node's I²t estimator
and must be measured. Locomotion is intermittent, so RMS leg current over a
gait is what matters, not a single continuous number.

At η = 0.85 the 4015 gives 2.27 continuous / **3.25** peak (the figure already
in the descriptor and `docs/BLUR_BRINGUP.md`); η = 0.75 is the conservative
read.

## Skills the real 4015 limits rule out

The 7 × 4015 cap abduction and the whole spine at **~2.0 N·m continuous /
~2.9 N·m peak** (vs the 3.8 the design assumes). These are exactly the 7
missing motors, so today the spine and abduction do not move at all.

- **Gallop / bound: RULED OUT.** A two-leg-stance bound needs spine S1
  6.6–11 N·m p95 (`BLUR/RESULTS_v36.md`); the 4015 peaks at ~2.9 N·m, 2–3×
  short. The spine, not leg speed, caps the gallop.
- **Table jump (750 mm): AT RISK.** It uses an abduction + spine push; the
  margin must be remeasured on the **final** body (`physics_checks.py` on
  BLUR_final), which has 9 % longer legs and a straighter stance than v36.
- **Walk: feasible but tight.** Spine p95 ≈ 2.12 N·m — within peak, but right
  at the continuous cap, so keep the training torque/energy penalty (brief
  §5: p95 torque ≤ 80 % of limit in normal conditions).
- **Sit / lie / stand-up / righting / balance: feasible.** Leg pitch/knee
  driven by the 2806.5 (4.0 N·m peak); abduction demand is modest.

## Where these numbers go

`torque_limits.json` is written for merge into
`BLUR_final/mujoco/blur_meta.json` (read-only source, so it is not edited in
place) and for the retargeted training env's current/torque caps. The
required-torque side (what each skill *demands*) must be recomputed on the
final model, not v36.
