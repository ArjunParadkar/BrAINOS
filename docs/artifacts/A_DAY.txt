
BrAInOS — a day in the life
───────────────────────────
  Not "does this work" but "can this be left running". Twenty thousand
  ticks of a body reporting telemetry, with rare faults, a human who
  speaks now and then, and quiet periods to consolidate in. The day is
  one I wrote, so it can show the loop is stable, affordable and
  attentive to the right things in a world with a known answer key —
  and cannot show what a real workshop is like.

  [ran         ] 20000 ticks, 95% of them quiet — the entity was not being asked questions, it was running
  [first notice] reflex stop: noticed on occurrence 1 · leg stall: noticed on occurrence 1
  [awareness   ] of 10 windows in which something went wrong, the human heard about it in 10 (100%) — not every occurrence, which would be the firehose, but every stretch of time
  [precision   ] 42 interruptions, 38 of them (90%) for something that deserved one
  [quiet       ] 155 things surfaced, 42 of which (27%) actually interrupted — 2 interruptions per thousand ticks. An entity that surfaces everything is a firehose nobody keeps; one that surfaces nothing is a machine that quietly fails
  [thinking    ] 691 escalations above the reflex tier, with 100% of the budget left at the end and 0 escalations the budget refused
  [dreaming    ] 0 consolidation passes during the day and 5 once the body was put away — a busy workshop never gives two hundred consecutive quiet ticks, and rewriting memory while a gait loop runs is the thing the quiet check exists to prevent. 201 memories at the end
  [by kind     ] quiet: 19244 seen, 17 reached, 0 interrupted · rail dip: 482 seen, 91 reached, 4 interrupted · leg stall: 54 seen, 14 reached, 5 interrupted · reflex stop: 33 seen, 33 reached, 33 interrupted · the human spoke: 187 seen, 0 reached, 0 interrupted
  [calibration ] surprise rate 1% — a well-calibrated entity in a familiar place should be low single digits, and one that is surprised half the time is somewhere it does not understand and should say so
  [verdict     ] this entity could be left running: it caught what mattered, stayed quiet, and still had budget at the end

Ten of those days, on the board Phase 0 runs on
───────────────────────────────────────────────
  §5 says the bound is a property of the body rather than of who the
  entity is. That is easy to write and worth checking, because an entity
  that notices what matters with four thousand episodes of room and
  misses it with two hundred and fifty-six is an entity whose attention
  was paid for by its memory — and Phase 0 runs on the small one.

  One day does not test it: at twenty thousand ticks the entity holds
  201 memories and the embedded bound of 256 never bites. So this is ten
  of them, which is where the small body starts forgetting.

                                    workstation       ESP32-S3
    memories at the end                    1983            260
    faults noticed on the first               1              1
    human aware of what mattered            98%            98%
    interrupt precision                     98%            98%
    interruptions per 1k ticks                2              2
    escalation budget left                 100%           100%
    could be left running                  true           true

  The board keeps 13% of what the workstation keeps and loses nothing in
  noticing. That is the claim §5 needs and it is not obvious: attention
  is a property of the ambient loop — the damping, the severity bands,
  consequence scaling evidence — rather than of how much is remembered.
  What the small body loses is *recall*, and it loses it in the order
  salience decides.
