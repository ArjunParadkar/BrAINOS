
BrAInOS — when the cloud goes away
──────────────────────────────────
  Stage 5 says where the mind runs is a deployment decision, not an
  identity decision. Reflexes go on the body because ten milliseconds is
  too slow to catch a fall; the frontier goes in a datacenter because it
  does not fit in 512 KiB of SRAM.

  That claim has a bill. If part of the mind is somewhere else, sometimes
  part of the mind is not there. The question is not whether the robot
  keeps working — the tier structure makes that easy. It is the one that
  hurts in the field: does anyone find out? A robot that silently drops
  from frontier reasoning to reflexes and carries on looking busy is
  worse than one that stops, because the results degrade in a way nobody
  attributes to the network for three weeks.

  [phase    ] everything reachable       ceiling frontier   · 60 situations, 15 escalations, 100% served at the tier they warranted (0 short on reach, 0 on budget) · 6 memories written
  [phase    ] datacenter unreachable     ceiling deliberate · 60 situations, 15 escalations, 0% served at the tier they warranted (15 short on reach, 0 on budget) · 6 memories written
  [phase    ] edge unreachable as well   ceiling fast       · 60 situations, 15 escalations, 0% served at the tier they warranted (15 short on reach, 0 on budget) · 6 memories written
  [phase    ] reach restored             ceiling frontier   · 60 situations, 15 escalations, 100% served at the tier they warranted (0 short on reach, 0 on budget) · 6 memories written
  [acting   ] the body kept moving through every phase: true · the reflex tier was never absent: true
  [recorded ] 2 outage(s) written into the entity's own memory, at the moment the reach dropped — §3.7 puts memory on the entity, so the record of the outage survives the outage
  [the fix  ] a degraded decision, as a human reads it: → deliberate (novel) [limited: out of reach] — the situation warranted frontier, the entity reached deliberate, and the reason is recorded as `out of reach`, not as an affordability problem

  The last line is the change this run was written to force. That field
  used to be one boolean called `budget_limited`, set whichever of budget
  or reach bit — so a dropped datacenter link was reported as an
  affordability problem. A human tops up the budget, nothing changes, and
  the robot goes on being quietly less capable than it was yesterday.
  They are different problems with different fixes and they now have
  different names.
